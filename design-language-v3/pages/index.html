Project Rio · design language v3 · measured off GYQE01

Three registers
and one token

Mario Superstar Baseball shipped a complete interface system in 2005. Read closely, it is not a style — it is three colour registers the game never mixes, and one symbol it says three different ways. Everything on this page traces to a texture id, a string-table row or a decomp symbol. Where something is our construction rather than the game's, it says so.

The law

One register per surface

Rio red on bright green was not too much colour. It was a register collision — loud treatment applied to chrome. The game does exactly the thing that was wrong, but only in two places, and it quarantines them. Getting this right is most of the work.

Chrome

Every container, gauge and label. Translucent black 50–71%, a 1–2px neutral rim, no bevel, no ornament. Outlines are the fill hue darkened, never black. Saturated fills are kept apart by a neutral band.

Loud

Stacked saturated hues with no separator, banded gradients on a dark keyline. The game uses it for minigame logos, place badges and result stamps — nowhere else. One element per page. The stamp here is this page's only one.

Map

Flat saturated colour with no outline at all. The challenge-map terrain, and nothing else. On the site this is for actual terrain — the visualizer's collision fills, a park diagram — never for UI.

The budget, written as a rule. A component library can enforce this: one loud slot per route, claimed by whatever matters most — a walk-off, an award, a title. The token

A bubble with a note in it

The game states chemistry three independent ways and they all agree. Tip #149 says "♪ is a symbol of friendship." A 3D emote pops over a linked player. A flat badge draws over each occupied base. Same bubble, same note. Its negative twin is a starburst with a scribble — the squiggle you remembered is real, it just belongs to anti-chemistry, which is why the burst read as invented when it was used as decoration.

Good · it pops and squashes

Pink bubble, fill #ffdeef, ring #ff94b5. Black eighth note with a white outline, rotated −30°. Two camera-facing quads — never the symbol alone, never the backing alone.

30f @ 30fps, then hold
scale 0 → 1.10 @f10 → 1.00 @f15
symbol −15° @f10
squash z 1.25 / y −0.18 @f18 → rest @f30

Anti · it shudders

Lilac 9-point starburst, fill #e7ceff, outline #ad4ad6. Dark purple scribble-swirl #310042, roughly three loops, rotated −20°.

30f @ 30fps, then hold
scale 0 → 1.133 @f10 → 0.80 @f20 → 1.00 @f30
symbol flickers −25° at f10 / f21 / f27
rests at f18 / f24 / f30
The asymmetry does the semantic work. Good overshoots and settles. Anti overshoots, sags, then recovers while twitching three times. Strip the colour out entirely and the two states are still distinguishable — which is the test a colour-coded pair usually fails. Both curves above are ported verbatim from the clips in entry 810 (prop/maxtame), sections 21 and 27, and run on steps(30) at 30fps because the originals are frame-stepped.

Said three ways

The HUD draws the same pairing flat. draw_OnBaseChemLinks puts element 0x11 (bubble, tex 0x118, 36×36) and 0x12 (note, tex 0x119) over each occupied base, bits 0/1/2 for first, second and third. The site inherits a live widget nothing currently shows.

Three states, never a ramp

The chemistry table thresholds at 90 and 20. Those are hard lines the game draws itself, so every chemistry display gets three bands and no gradient. Saturated rim, neutral core: only the extremes carry hue.

The table is 0x36 bytes per character at CharacterStats+0x3B, 54 rows, and it is perfectly symmetric — 0 of 1431 pairs disagree. It renders as a triangle, not a square. With 101 bright and 53 hostile pairs against a mean of 59.9, it has real structure, which makes it the best data visual on the disc.

Stadiums

A material and a hazard, then a colour

Seven accent colours is not seven themes. Each park is a short list of nameable materials and one thing that lives there — both of which the game states outright. The fills below are procedural CSS recipes built from generic surfaces (brick courses, ashlar, plank, dune ripple, mow stripes, starfield), so a theme can carry real texture without shipping any extracted art. Hazards are verbatim from the loading tips.

Extend, don't replace

stadiumThemes.ts is colour-only today — sky, fog, hemi, sun, an 11-key collision palette. Add a materials object, a voice string and a motifs list of recipe kinds, not image paths. The existing labels stay: "Molten Keep" and "Picture-Book Meadow" are doing real work.

Two copies of this theme disagree right now. stadiumThemes.ts has drifted from RioVisualizer/web/themes.js — Mario's fog density is 0.0028 against 0.0014, Bowser's hemi and sun are completely different (#5a3a32/0.7 vs #8c88a8/1.3), and Wario differs on six keys. Pick a direction and write down which wins, or the overlay and the match page end up looking like different products.
Chrome, in detail

The container is almost nothing

This is the correction that reshapes the whole package. The earlier "plaque" — hard dark outline, top bevel, bottom shadow — is not what the game draws. Its panel is a translucent black rectangle with a rim you can barely see, and the content does all the work. Every card on this page is that panel.

Gauges

Capsules, 9–30px wide, fully rounded ends, a light top cap, a continuous fill, a darker rail behind. No ticks. No segments. Labels float beside them — white text, dark outline, no pill, no chip, no plate.

Focus is a translucent bar

Not a glow and not a bracket. The cursor is a rounded rectangle assembled from three slices — a cap, a repeating middle and a mirrored cap — which is why its pieces look like fragments in a texture dump. Black at 57% behind a warm three-row rim, rounded about 5px.

Superstars Off · Season 7 Superstars On · Season 7 Big Balla
Two corrections here, not one. This package first said focus was a pair of gold brackets, then four white corners mirrored from 1745:037. Both were wrong. Worse, I read 1745:027 and 028 as a recessed "slot and lip" and built a principle on it — one mechanical object, for bounded resources. Those two textures are this bar's middle and cap. There is no slot, and that rule is withdrawn.

Quantise every gradient

The game's gradients step in 1–2px bands. Hard stops, five to seven of them. Almost free to implement, and no other esports site does it — because it looks like a mistake unless it is deliberate and consistent.

Colour lives on the edge

Results rings 2516:001–005 are a 4px coloured ring around a disc of black at 85%. All five differ only in rim hue. That is the mechanism that lets seven park themes and forty-eight team palettes coexist without fighting.

Loading

Seventeen tips, already written

The part worth pushing hardest on. The disc ships seventeen loading tips with banner titles and bodies, and the frame they sit in is already right: a dimmed park, outlined lettering, a halftone vignette closing the edges. Rio's own tips should be written the same way and sit in the same place — and the swing character should rotate with context, the captain whose page is loading or who won the game being opened.

What's chemistry?
♪ is a symbol of friendship. Buddies exhibit unpredictable powers when they field and bat.

The swing is real

Bowser's body is entry 262, his batting bank 264, the clip b09_ba_008 — his two-second charge swing. Exported as rigged glTF with parts="bat", which swaps his hands into their bat pose and brings the spiked mace with them, then rendered to sixteen orthographic frames and run on steps(16). 207×150 per frame, 203 KB, quantised to 128 colours, which is how the game stores its own UI textures.

The copy register, from the settings screen

Short, imperative, a dash between label and explanation, no marketing voice. Three verbatim examples:

"Mercy — End a game if the score differential reaches 10 runs."

"Innings — Set how many innings you'll play. When a game is in a tie, you can play up to 3 extra innings."

"Players will join you when you get all their flags."

And 32 finished character bios. Written in the game's own voice, sitting on the disc right now, while the character dashboards ship with placeholder writeups waiting on community content. They won't cover the Rio-specific analysis, but they end the empty state immediately. Mario's, in full: "Everyone's favorite hero. When Peach is in trouble, he always saves the day! A kart racer, tennis player, golf enthusiast, doctor… The list goes on and on, showing he's a jack-of-all-trades. His trademark Fireball will help him in his first foray into baseball."
Teams

A pennant is a device, not a template

The game ships 48 team names in three renderings each — 144 textures, not 144 teams. Index is captain*4 + slot: wordmarks on their own silhouettes at 153×54, emblems at 90×90, text strips at 250×26. Each wordmark has its own shape, so a pennant per team is the wrong read. The pennant is a section device — a standings banner, a bracket header, a league marker — and the per-team identity is carried by the wordmark and emblem the game already drew.

Team Stars are a count, not a theme

Five 13×13 orange orbs per team in an orange frame (element 0x18, tex 0x115) — a finished live-scoreboard widget the site doesn't show. But a count is all it is, and it shouldn't become a site-wide motif.

Pips, not digits

Balls, strikes and outs are drawn as three, two and two pips — always one fewer than the count that ends the state (element 0x107). A box score that renders them as pips reads as this game's scoreboard instantly, and as a generic one if it renders digits.

The change

What goes, and what replaces it

Drop

Including things this package itself got wrong
  • Every invented mushroom. The real mark is already a 16×16 three-colour pixel logo with a mono SVG companion. It needed no redrawing and gets none. mushroom-pixel.svg should come out of public/images/.
  • The shell mark and the loose burst. Neither was sourced. The burst is real but it belongs to anti-chemistry and has one negative job.
  • Heavy plaques — hard outline, top bevel, drop shadow. The game's container is translucent black with a hairline rim.
  • Coloured accent rails on cards, the clearest template signature there is.
  • A pennant per team. The pennant is a section device; the team identity is the wordmark.
  • Letter grades and star ratings. No grade exists anywhere on the disc.
  • Glow on focus. There is none on the disc, anywhere.
  • Smooth gradients. The game's are banded in 1–2px steps.
  • Team Stars as a site-wide theme. They're a live count, not a motif.

Keep

Measured, with a source
  • One register per surface, and one loud element per page.
  • The bubble and the note, with its shuddering twin — the one token the game says three different ways.
  • Per-park materials and hazards, as procedural recipes, so themes carry texture without shipping art.
  • Loading tips, in the game's own frame and voice, with the swing rotating by context.
  • Banded gradients and saturated rim, neutral core.
  • Capsule gauges with no ticks and floating labels.
  • The game's nouns — Past Superstars, Star Chance, Buddy players, Big Plays.
  • #0c2424 as the ground, present in all seven parks at ~4.5%.
  • The 16-cell logo grid as the 4px spacing unit.

Provenance. Colour, geometry and timing measured from textures, models and string tables extracted with MSSB-Editor from Mario Superstar Baseball (USA).iso (GYQE01), read in place. Symbol names and draw logic from mssb-dtk. Chemistry data from the roster table in entry 7. Tips and vocabulary verbatim from text tables T79 and T83. The Rio logo files are the project's own.

Honest limits. Much of this is decoded rather than observed. No HUD frame-to-texture mapping was verified by running the game; the emotes were exported and measured but never rendered in motion; the two emote symbol outlines are our constructions, and the focus treatment was wrong twice before the selection bar was found. Table figures are illustrative.

Asset policy. The park fills here are procedural CSS over generic materials specifically so themes carry texture without redistributing Nintendo art. The wordmarks and emblems are a separate decision — the frontend already ships 48 emblems today, while PRSH takes the stricter line because it redistributes as an installer.

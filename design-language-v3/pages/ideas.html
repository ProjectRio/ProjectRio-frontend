Every idea here is tied to a real route in the frontend and a real thing the game does. Nothing is a mood or a vibe — each one names its source so you can check it. Tick the ones you want and copy the shortlist out at the end. Three Registers is the law; this is the menu.

How to read the tags. READY means we already have everything needed — data, art and API. DATA needs a backend field or an endpoint that doesn't exist yet. ART needs something drawn or extracted. YOUR CALL is blocked on a decision only you can make. FIX is a defect, not an enhancement.

Provenance. Sources are texture ids from Mario Superstar Baseball (USA).iso (GYQE01) read in place with MSSB-Editor, symbol names from mssb-dtk, strings from text tables T79 and T83, and routes from this repo's app/ tree. Where an idea rests on something we inferred rather than read, the source line says so.

This is a menu, not a plan. A hundred-odd ideas is far more than should be built — the point is to choose. Ticks are stored in this browser only; they are not shared and they do not reach me unless you paste the shortlist back.
